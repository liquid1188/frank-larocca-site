Ahead of the December 8, 2018 premiere at St. Mary's Cathedral, Catholic Arts Today spoke with Frank about the making of the *Mass of the Americas*: Archbishop Cordileone's idea of a twinned tribute to Our Lady of Guadalupe and the Immaculate Conception, the Mexican hymns woven through it, and the first Ave Maria set in Nahuatl. Read the Institute's three-part profile, [Frank La Rocca's Search for the Sacred](https://benedictinstitute.org/2018/06/frank-la-roccas-search-sacred/), and the [Mass of the Americas page](https://benedictinstitute.org/mass-of-the-americas-composed-by-frank-la-rocca/).
