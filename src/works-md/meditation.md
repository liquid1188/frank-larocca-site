Meditation is a brief ruminative, introspective essay written as a tribute to my friend and colleague Robert Basart (to whom it is dedicated) shortly before he died. It is intensely private music, and its intimate character may put one in mind of a quiet exchange between old friends. Meditation was commissioned by Fallen Leaf Press as part of its "Various Leaves" anthology.

<p class="note">From the composer's notes to <em>In This Place</em> (Enharmonic Records, 2013).</p>
