In progress. Commissioned by Archbishop Cordileone for 2027, the centenary of Pope Benedict's birth and the fifth anniversary of his death, to be celebrated at the close of the Institute's June 2027 conference on his legacy at St. Patrick's Seminary. Of its seven or eight movements the Dies Irae and several others are complete; two or three remain to be written.
