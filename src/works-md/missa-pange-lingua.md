A paraphrase Mass on the lesser-known Corpus Christi chant, commissioned by Archbishop Cordileone and first sung on Pentecost 2024 at the Cathedral of St. Mary of the Assumption, San Francisco, at the Mass that opened the St. Junípero Serra route of the National Eucharistic Pilgrimage. The Kyrie and Agnus Dei invite the congregation to sing.
