A Requiem for the displaced and the homeless, written for the Benedict XVI Institute and sung each November at Masses for those who have died on the streets. Recorded with the Messe des Malades by the Benedict XVI Choir and Orchestra under Richard Sparks.

Drawing on his Ukrainian heritage, the Requiem includes a setting of a poem on the Ukrainian priest Andrei Ischak, martyred by the Bolsheviks, and has been sung to pray for Ukrainian refugees.
